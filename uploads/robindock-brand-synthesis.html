01 — The Idea
Two registers, one system

RobinDock is a document platform that two very different people rely on: the owner or office manager who has to trust it with patient records, and the tech or front-desk staffer who lives in it all shift. The brand serves both by running in two modes built from the same palette, type, and shapes — so it always reads as one product.

Mode A — default
Calm

Light, airy, legible

Pale gradient washes, white surfaces, hairlines, big calm headlines, generous space. Apple-clean. Nothing competes with the content.

Use for: the entire product UI, most of the marketing page, anything read or worked in for long stretches.
Mode B — accent
Confident ·

Bold color blocks, platform presence

Deep indigo or royal blocks, the "three clouds" framing, bold stat bands. Salesforce-style confidence — used sparingly, for impact.

Use for: the network/Flock story, pricing, marketing hero accents, dashboards — moments selling the platform vision.
The rule: calm is the default and carries ~80% of every surface. Confident mode is a deliberate accent — a few moments per marketing page, and in-product only on dashboards or the Flock view, never the document-triage screens.
What keeps it one brand: identical palette, Inter type, pill/card shapes, and the robin mark across both modes. Only the intensity changes, never the ingredients.

03 — The Three Clouds
Product structure = color system

RobinDock's platform is framed as three "clouds," and each owns one accent color. This is the bridge between the two modes: on marketing it's bold cloud cards; in-product it's the same colors as quiet tags, top-bars, and dots. One mental model, head to tail.

Document Cloud

Capture & organize. Royal blue.

Patient Cloud

Tag & timeline. Violet.

Flock Network

Exchange between practices. Aqua.

Why it works: a referral that arrives via the Flock gets an aqua tag; a patient timeline accent is violet; a primary "Tag to pet" button is royal. The user learns the color language without being taught it — and the marketing site's bold cloud cards reinforce the exact same code.

06 — Rules
Do & don't
Do
  • Keep the product in calm mode — light surfaces, the document is the hero.
  • Reserve indigo/royal blocks for the Flock, pricing, dashboards, and marketing accents.
  • Tie each accent to its meaning: royal=action, violet=patient, aqua=network.
  • Let headlines carry weight through scale, not decoration.
  • Use generous whitespace and hairlines to keep dense screens calm.
Don't
  • Drop dark indigo blocks into document-triage or list screens.
  • Use violet and aqua interchangeably — they carry different meaning.
  • Stack multiple bold color blocks back-to-back; one accent moment at a time.
  • Add a second typeface or decorative display font.
  • Let confident mode exceed ~20% of any surface.
On the logo: the placeholder robin mark (royal square, aqua "breast" detail) stands in until trademark clearance and a designer produce the real mark. The wordmark is Inter 700. Whatever the final mark, it should read cleanly at 24px in a nav and hold up in a single color.
